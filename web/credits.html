WCAG and ARRM

WCAG 2.2 is a W3C Recommendation. Understanding documents are informative W3C material. W3C content remains governed by the WAI reuse permissions and the WCAG 2.2 Recommendation.

ARRM tasks and roles are attributed to W3C and the ARRM contributors. See the ARRM task listing, ARRM roles and acknowledgements. ARRM is advisory responsibility guidance, not a conformance standard.
